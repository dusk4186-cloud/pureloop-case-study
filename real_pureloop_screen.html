UX / UI CASE STUDY

PureLoop
Refresh. Rinse. Repeat.

Laundry made effortless.

A comprehensive UX/UI case study for a mobile application that bridges the gap between residents of hostels, PGs, and flats with reliable laundry & ironing service providers in Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities across India.

15+ Screens Designed
7 Core User Flows
3 User Personas
INTRODUCTION & BACKGROUND

Key Challenges Addressed

🔍 Discoverability

Residents struggle to find reliable laundry services nearby, especially in unfamiliar cities.

⏰ Time Management

Busy schedules of students and professionals leave little time for manual laundry coordination.

💸 Price Transparency

Lack of upfront pricing makes it hard to compare and budget for laundry services.

📦 Order Tracking

No visibility into the status of clothes — pickup, washing, ironing, and delivery stages.

⭐ Trust & Quality

Difficulty assessing provider quality without ratings, reviews, or verified profiles.

💳 Payment Hassle

Cash-only models and lack of digital payment options create friction in transactions.
